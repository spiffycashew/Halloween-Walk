    The Playful Park of Peculiar

    • Have your booking email open. The queue forms just inside the main gates.
    • The one-way trail takes about 30 minutes. Food and drink stalls are near the end.
    • No toilets in the park. The Design Museum next to the gate is the nearest.
    • You leave at the top of the park, at the end of the cycle path. Route 2 starts there.
